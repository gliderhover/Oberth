03FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Which GPUs can I lease?
NVIDIA H100, H200, B200 and B300, delivered as 8-GPU nodes.
What contract lengths are available?
Long-term reservations of 3 to 5 years, and short terms from 1 month to 1 year.
When is capacity available?
Reserved US capacity is available from January 2027. If you need an earlier date, include it in your request and we will check what is possible.
Where is the capacity located?
In the United States.
Can teams outside the US lease capacity?
Yes, where US export rules allow it. Tell us where your team is based when you send a request.
Will you sign an NDA?
Yes. We are happy to sign a mutual NDA before either side shares specifics.
How do I get started?
Use the capacity matcher above, book a 30-minute call, or email daniel@oberth.org with your GPU type, size, start date and term.
